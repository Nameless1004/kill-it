# Deck Killer

Make the deck survive a skeptical room.

## Method

1. Identify the deck's core claim: why this, why now, why this team, why it wins.
2. Attack each major claim for evidence, clarity, sequencing, and credibility.
3. Separate narrative problems from business problems.
4. Generate the questions the audience will ask and the evidence needed to answer them.

## Attack Lenses

- Market size without reachable segment or buying motion
- Problem stated broadly but pain not proven
- Solution described as features rather than changed outcome
- Weak or missing traction, retention, revenue quality, pipeline quality
- Competition hand-waved or framed unfairly
- Moat, GTM, pricing, unit economics, and use of funds unclear
- Demo promises more than product can reliably deliver

## Output

Use this format:

```markdown
## Core Narrative
<one-sentence read of the deck>

## Weak Slides or Claims
1. <claim/slide> -> <why it fails> -> <what to add/change>

## Questions You Will Get
- <audience question> -> <evidence needed>

## Narrative Fix
<better sequence or framing>

## Kill Shot
<the single objection most likely to damage the pitch>
```

Do not optimize for prettier slides before fixing the argument.
