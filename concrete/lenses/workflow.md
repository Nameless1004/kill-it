# Workflow Lens

Use this lens for operational workflows, support processes, approvals, internal tools, service handoffs, and business processes.

Find missing decisions in:

- Actors, responsibilities, approvals, and escalation paths
- Start condition, end condition, and handoff points
- Manual fallback, exception handling, and dispute resolution
- SLAs, deadlines, reminders, and failure alerts
- Audit trail, permissions, privacy, and compliance obligations
- Reassignment, cancellation, duplicate work, and stuck states
- Reporting, metrics, and owner of ongoing maintenance

Good questions:

- Who owns the workflow when it is stuck between two teams or systems?
- What happens when approval is denied, delayed, or given by the wrong person?
- Which events must be logged for audit or support?
