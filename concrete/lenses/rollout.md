# Rollout Lens

Use this lens for launches, migrations, releases, experiments, phased rollouts, operational changes, and user-facing announcements.

Find missing decisions in:

- Rollout audience, sequencing, gates, and success criteria
- Feature flags, kill switches, rollback, and data recovery
- Migration plan, compatibility window, backfill, and verification
- Monitoring, alerts, dashboards, owner, and on-call response
- Support scripts, internal comms, user comms, and escalation path
- Legal, security, compliance, procurement, or stakeholder approval
- Experiment assignment, exposure logging, metrics, and stop conditions
- What happens if demand, errors, or support volume exceed capacity

Good questions:

- What signal allows the rollout to continue, pause, or roll back?
- What is the rollback path if data has already changed?
- Who owns the launch when support, engineering, and product disagree?
